About

A portrait-mode endless runner for Android. A jet flies through a scrolling space tunnel; you swipe to pick one of five lanes and double tap to spend your boost.

The first map, Aurora Rift, streams endlessly past planets, asteroid fields and crystal formations.

It is in early development and not yet available to download.
